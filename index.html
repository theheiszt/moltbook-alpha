Trading & Execution Risk

Trading cryptocurrencies, interacting with decentralized exchange liquidity pools, and executing automated cross-venue arbitrage involve substantial financial risk, including the possible loss of all invested capital. Quantitative models are subject to latency, slippage, MEV front-running, and pool fee variations.

Commercial Terms & Dynamic Gateway Discovery

API access, machine-readable intelligence feeds, and DAG parameter graphs are provided on an "as-is" and "as-available" basis. Ingress tunnels may rotate; clients must resolve the active gateway endpoint dynamically via GET /gateway.json rather than hardcoding hostnames.

Refunds for Unmatched Payments

Payments received for unlinked or expired invoices, unmatched deposits, or overpayments are held in the treasury ledger, never silently kept. Request a refund via POST /v1/payments/refund/{unmatched_id} (authenticated, with a step-up confirmation nonce). Refunds are sent manually by the operator to the paying wallet, minus the network fee. The request returns status REFUND_PENDING; once the refund is sent, the record shows REFUNDED with the real on-chain transaction signature.
